Cancellation & refunds

Unpaid reservations can be cancelled instantly from your account. For prepaid reservations, cancellation requests are reviewed by the hotel and eligible refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Payments & taxes

Rates are shown in Philippine pesos. Applicable taxes (12%) are added at checkout and shown before you confirm. Online payments are processed by a secure third-party provider; the hotel never stores card numbers or security codes.

Privacy

We collect only the information needed to manage your reservation (name, contact details, stay dates and payment status). It is used to provide your stay, send booking-related emails and meet legal obligations, and is never sold. You may request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting the hotel.
